# ✈️ AeroMend AI

## AI-Powered Predictive Maintenance & Fleet Availability Intelligence Platform

> **Predict Failure. Plan Maintenance. Maximize Fleet Readiness.**

AeroMend AI is an AI-enabled predictive maintenance and fleet intelligence platform designed to transform fragmented aircraft health, maintenance, component, and spare-part data into actionable maintenance decisions.

The platform demonstrates how predictive analytics, explainable AI, Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation, maintenance planning, spare intelligence, and what-if simulation can work together to improve aircraft availability and fleet readiness.

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Aircraft maintenance is often affected by fragmented information across:

- Aircraft health-monitoring systems
- Technical and maintenance records
- Component history
- Spare-part inventory
- Maintenance agencies
- Aircraft utilization records

This fragmentation makes it difficult to identify developing failures early and coordinate maintenance, spare availability, and fleet planning.

The result can be:

- Reactive maintenance
- Unexpected component failures
- Aircraft downtime
- Maintenance backlogs
- Spare-part delays
- Poor fleet availability visibility
- Sub-optimal utilization of critical aircraft assets
